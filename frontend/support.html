Frequently Asked Questions

Is my medical data kept private? ▾

Yes. Reports are securely stored and are intended to be accessible only through your account. MediDecode does not share your data with third parties without appropriate consent.

Which file types can I upload? ▾

You can upload PDF reports or JPG/PNG images of medical reports. You can also capture a photo directly using a supported device.

How accurate is the simplified explanation? ▾

MediDecode uses OCR and medical reference information to help explain medical terms and values in simpler language. The information is for understanding and should not be treated as a medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

Can I change my language later? ▾

Yes. You can change your preferred language from the Settings section at any time.

How do I delete a report? ▾

Open Report History, find the report you want to remove, and use the available delete action.
